# Agency client acquisition

**REQUIRED:** Follow texau-operator. Source with local-maps-leads, google-web-research, or linkedin-specialist. Send with linkedin-outreach or lead-gen-expert hand-off.

## Overview

Agencies win with proof that the prospect has a specific problem the agency fixes. Every lead needs one observed problem. Every first message names that problem and offers a small free fix or finding.

## Pick the playbook by service

| Service | Find prospects | Observed problem | Opener |
|---|---|---|---|
| Web design | Maps by category and city | Website empty, or no mobile meta tags | "Your listing gets calls but has no site to send them to." |
| SEO | Google search for the niche and city | Not in the top 10 for their own service plus city, or missing title and schema | The query they miss and who ranks instead |
| Paid ads | Maps or search domains | No Meta or Google pixel | "You cannot retarget visitors today." |
| Reputation | Maps | Rating under 4.0 or few reviews | Their review count next to the top 3 competitors |
| Social media | LinkedIn company pages or Maps | No posts in 60 days (Company Activity Extractor) | The date of the last post |
| Lead gen or automation | LinkedIn Job Search Export | Hiring SDRs or ops roles | The role they are hiring for and what it costs |
| Recruiting agency | LinkedIn Job Search Export | Same role open 30+ days | The open role and its age |

## Pipeline

1. Ask for the service, the niche, the area, and the deal size. The service row in the table above is the qualifier. Do not ask for it again.
   Research depth by monthly deal size: under $1,000, the evidence column only. $1,000 to $5,000, add one competitor comparison. Over $5,000, add the mini audit in step 6.
2. Size the source from the target backwards. For Maps leads, use the yields in local-maps-leads.
3. Collect the evidence for the observed problem as a column. A lead without evidence is dropped.
4. Find the decision maker. For local businesses, look for the owner on the website or with LinkedIn Company Employees Export. For B2B, look for the marketing or growth lead. If no name is found, greet the team by company name.
5. Write the opener from the evidence column. Show the user 3 samples first. If the user only asked for drafts, deliver them as a CSV column and send nothing.
6. For a high deal size, add a mini audit per lead: screenshot, two numbers, one fix.

## Common mistakes

- Generic "we help businesses grow" copy with no observed problem.
- Pitching a business that has the problem because it is closed or tiny. Filter by review count or headcount first.
- Sending the full audit cold. Send one finding and offer the rest.
